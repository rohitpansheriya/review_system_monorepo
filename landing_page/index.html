FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do customers need to download any application? ▾
No! AppNexa runs entirely on the customer's native mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) via contactless NFC or camera QR scan.
How does private feedback routing work? ▾
When a customer selects 1 to 3 stars, they are routed to share their feedback privately on WhatsApp. This lets you catch unhappy feedback privately so you can fix it directly with the customer.
Are the Google reviews 100% genuine and compliant? ▾
Yes! AppNexa redirects customers directly to their own logged-in Google Maps account where they post authentic reviews. Every review comes from a real customer who visited your store and posted from their own Google account.
